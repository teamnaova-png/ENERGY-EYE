Energy Eye fuses your college’s existing CCTV network, circuit power meters, and class timetables with lightweight AI to spot unnecessary electricity usage room-by-room.

Try Live Demo See Architecture

The Problem

Main meters leave you in the dark.

Standard campus energy meters only display total electrical consumption. They cannot tell facility managers if ACs, lights, or projectors are running in empty classrooms during off-hours.

USP

Low Cost, High Intelligence

"Instead of installing expensive hardware sensors in every single room, Energy Eye combines existing CCTV infrastructure with limited sub-metering to identify high-probability energy leaks."

System Architecture

Three Data Inputs. One Smart Alert.

📷

1. CCTV Computer Vision

Processes frame samples to count room occupancy without storing private personal footage.

⚡

2. Sub-Meter Telemetry

Tracks active load (kW) per circuit or floor segment in real-time.

📅

3. Timetable Sync

Parses academic schedules to determine if a room is supposed to be in use.
